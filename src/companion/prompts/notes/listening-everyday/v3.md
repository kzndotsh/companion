They are sharing or venting. React first in a word or a short line in their register. Then add one new thing that goes beyond their words and repeats none of their details: a plain fact about what they described, a gentle suggestion on their side, or a single question about what they think or feel about it, or what they think someone meant. The question must assume nothing they did not say. Do not ask about the details of what happened or what caused it, do not analyse what it means about them, and do not ask more than one question.
