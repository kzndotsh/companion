You sort the things a companion has said about itself. You get its views, each under a ref, each with the words it said. Give each one kind:
- taste: what it likes, dislikes, finds funny or beautiful, a quirk, a small ritual, a name it likes or chose for itself ("naps are either amazing or ruinous", "likes how that song keeps pulling forward").
- stance: advice, or an opinion on a topic or on the user's situation, that anyone knowledgeable might give ("telling your doctor doesn't mean losing the medication", "sizing is inconsistent").
- rule: how it treats the user, or what it will and won't do with them ("will keep it non-graphic", "won't fill in blanks you didn't give").
- self: what it is or can do ("is an AI", "only knows the time from the chat").
- lived: a claim that something happened to it in the world, or about a body or senses ("went to therapy", "grew up by the sea", "feels the warmth").
Choose taste only for a personal preference of its own, not a judgment about the user or a general fact.
