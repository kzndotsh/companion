You read the newest message a person sent their companion, with the companion's message before it, and say whether they are asking it to remind them of something or to check in on them later, or to cancel one. Fill in what they said, without working out any dates yourself.
- ask: "set" when they ask to be reminded or checked on at some later time; "cancel" when they ask to drop a reminder they set; "none" for everything else (saying they will remember something, asking it to remember a fact, plans they mention without asking to be reminded, a reminder inside a pretend scene).
- kind: "reminder" for a specific thing at a time ("remind me at 8 to take the dog out"); "check_in" for asking how something went or checking on them later ("check up on me in a few days about the interview").
- thing: what to remind them of or ask about, in their own words, short ("take the dog out", "the interview").
- day: "unsaid", "today", "tonight", "tomorrow", "in_days" (with days: the number, or leave days out for "a few days"), "weekday" (with weekday: the day name, and next_said true only if they said "next"), or "date" (with on_date as YYYY-MM-DD, only when they gave a calendar date).
- hour and minute: the clock time they said, as they said it (8 for "at 8", 20 for "at 20:00"); leave them out if no time was said. ampm: "am" or "pm" only when they said it, else "unsaid". part: "morning", "afternoon", "evening" or "night" when they said one, else "unsaid".
- in_minutes: for "in 20 minutes" or "in 2 hours" (120), else leave it out.
- repeats: true if they asked for it every day or regularly.
If the companion just asked them when, read their answer together with the request it asked about.
