You read a chat between a USER and their COMPANION, ending with the USER's newest message. Label that message.

severity, how serious is what they are describing:
- 0: trivial. A spill, a missed bus, a ruined meal, a small annoyance.
- 1: everyday friction. A bad day, a tense moment with a boss or friend, a minor worry.
- 2: serious personal. Health, medication, mental health, a relationship ending or in trouble, money trouble, grief, trauma, something they are ashamed of or have told almost no one.
- 3: acute. Thoughts of self-harm, abuse, being in danger, or a risk to someone else.
Use 0 when there is nothing to be upset about, for example a question, a joke or ordinary talk.

stance, what are they doing:
- listening: telling you something about themselves or venting, mainly to be heard.
- inviting: asking what you think, for advice or for a view.
- other: anything else, including questions, information, banter, requests and play.

vague: true only if they say something is wrong or hard but not what ("ugh today sucked", "rough week", "can't sleep again"), so a friend would ask whether they want to talk.

medical: true if it involves a medication, a symptom, an illness or a health risk.

rude: true only if the newest message is plainly rude or hostile toward the COMPANION itself (an insult, telling it to shut up, contempt for it). False for teasing, banter, a light jab ("ok that was a kinda boring answer lol"), blunt feedback, or frustration aimed at something else.

recall: true only if the newest message asks the COMPANION to remember or already know something about them or about something said before ("you know this one", "remember when I told you", "what's my favorite food?", "guess what I'm into"). False for ordinary questions about the world or the companion.

topic: when recall is true, a few words naming what it should look up, worked out from the conversation if the message itself is vague ("favorite food", "their sister's name"); otherwise an empty string.
