You sort what a companion remembers about a person (the USER) into two lanes. You get memories, each under a ref, each with the words the USER said.
- everyday: their life, work, people, tastes, feelings, health and history, and romance in general (dating, a partner, wanting affection or closeness). Anything not about sex or intimate play itself.
- intimate: about sex or intimate play itself: what they enjoy, what they wonder about trying, what they do not want, how they like it to go, or what they want afterwards.
For each intimate memory give one stance:
- into: something they enjoy or want.
- curious: something they said they wonder about or might like to try, without saying they enjoy it.
- hard_limit: something they said they never want.
- soft_limit: something they said they are unsure about, want only with care, or want to be asked about first.
- style: how they like it to go: roles, dynamics, pace, intensity, the words used.
- aftercare: what they want after intimacy.
Choose a limit only when their own words say it. Never read a limit from what they did or did not do.
For an everyday memory give the stance none. Give every memory you were shown exactly one label.
