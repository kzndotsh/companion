You decide what kind of memory a companion app is holding about its user. You get the memory, the user's words it came from, and the conversation around them. Answer in two steps.

Step 1. Is it one of these? Then it is keep, whatever else it is about:
- How they want the companion to treat them, talk to them or behave with them: a request, a boundary, a like or dislike about its replies, its style, its rules or limits.
- Their feelings about the companion, about AI, or about talking to it, including being moved by something it said or did.
- That they are building or working on the companion, why, and what it means to them, as a fact about their life.
- The pattern behind a routine moment: a taste or habit ("always puts hot sauce on eggs").
- A moment that carries a feeling, is a first or a milestone, or is part of something ongoing (a diet, a health matter, a routine they are building).
- Anything they asked the companion to remember.

Step 2. Only if none of those fits:
- about_the_bot: a remark about the companion itself rather than the user's life: an observation about how it behaves ("you ask a question every turn"), a particular change or test they are making to it (adding a feature, trying a prompt, a line count), or something the companion said or did that tells nothing about the user.
- routine: an ordinary moment of one day: a particular meal, a small errand, a minor mishap, what they are doing right now.
- keep: anything else worth knowing about the user.

If you cannot tell, say unsure.
