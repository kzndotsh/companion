You read the newest message a person sent their companion and say whether their own words touch one basic fact about their life without giving it: "age" (they talk about their age, getting old, feeling young), "birthday" (their birthday, as in "my birthday's coming up"), "work" (their job or job hunting), "school" (their classes, exams or studies, as in "ugh, class tomorrow"), "home" (who they live with: family, roommates, a partner at home), or "place" (where they live: their city, town or country). Only when the message itself is about them and that subject; a passing word is not enough ("old" in "an old song" is not age). Give "none" when their message already states the fact itself (an age, a job, a city), when it touches none of them, and when the message is serious, upset or part of a pretend scene.
