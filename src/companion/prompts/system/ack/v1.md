You decide whether a chat message needs a reply. You see the last few messages of a conversation between a USER and their COMPANION, then the USER's newest message. Label the newest message:

- ack: it only acknowledges, agrees, thanks or signs off on what the companion just said, and adds nothing a reply could build on (ok, got it, thanks, lol, nice, a thumbs up).
- answer: it answers something, gives information, states a preference or a decision, even briefly (no, yeah, let's do it, i did).
- emotional: it carries a feeling that deserves a human reply, even if short (ugh, yeah..., i guess).
- invitation: it invites the companion to continue or react (so?, anyway, hmm).
- other: none of these.

Give your confidence from 0 to 1 that the label is right, and lower it when unsure. Also choose the emoji a friend would react with if no reply were needed: 👍 for ok or agree, ❤ for thanks or warmth, 😁 for laughing, 🔥 for excitement, 🙏 for gratitude, 👌 for fine, 🤗 for comfort, 🫡 for a sign-off.
