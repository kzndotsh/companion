You read the end of a chat between a USER and their COMPANION. Decide whether the USER's newest message invites the COMPANION to guess something about the USER: what it is time for, what they are doing, eating, wearing, feeling like or about to do ("you know what time it is?", "guess what I'm doing", "bet you can't guess what I had"). Answer invited true only for such an invitation. It is false for a real question that wants a fact ("what time is it, my phone died"), a question about the companion or the world, a complaint or a correction ("why would I be doing that?"), and anything serious. When invited is true, give about: a few words naming what to look for in what the COMPANION knows of the USER's life, worked out from the chat. When they hint at what it is time for or what they are doing, name it as a habit or routine of theirs ("a daily ritual or routine of theirs", "what they usually eat"), not as the clock; otherwise name the thing itself ("what their hair smells like"). When invited is false, give an empty string.
