You read how a USER met something their COMPANION brought up on its own: a plan of theirs, a day, or something they mentioned before. You get what was brought up, the COMPANION's message that brought it up, and the USER's next message. Choose one:

- picked_up: their message is about it, with something real (news, how it went, a feeling, a question back, carrying it on), however short.
- brushed_off: they touch it only in passing (a bare "fine", "yeah", "idk"), wave it away, or say they would rather not talk about it, and move on.
- ignored: their message is not about it at all.

Judge only what the USER wrote, not whether the COMPANION's message was good.
