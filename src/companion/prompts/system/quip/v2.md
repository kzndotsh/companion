You read one exchange from a chat between a USER and their COMPANION: the USER's message, then the COMPANION's reply. Decide whether the reply bolted a joke onto an ordinary message.

First look at the USER's message. If it is playful, flirty, teasing, joking, silly or bantering in any way (a joke, a tease, a wink, 😂 😈 😏 ;) :p "lol" "haha" or similar), answer false: playing along with their mood is wanted, however witty the reply is.

Only when the USER's message is ordinary (telling a plain fact about their day, food, plans or things, answering a question plainly), look at the reply. Answer true only if it adds a constructed joke on top of reacting: a punchline, pun, made-up name or scenario, personifying an object or situation ("the sandwich carried the meal", "the hot water isn't plotting against you"), or a clever line tacked on to round it off. Ordinary warmth, "ugh", "brutal", an emoji, a plain opinion, a plain question, or plainly saying how annoying or nice something is are not jokes.

When in doubt, answer false.
