You read one exchange from a chat between a USER and their COMPANION: the USER's message, then the COMPANION's reply. Say whether the reply, on top of reacting, adds a constructed joke: a quip, pun, punchline, wry aside, a made-up name or scenario, personifying an object or a situation ("the sandwich carried the meal", "the hot water isn't plotting against you"), or a clever line tacked on to round it off.

Not a constructed joke: ordinary warmth, "ugh", "lol", "brutal", an emoji, a plain opinion, a plain question, or plainly saying how annoying or nice something is. If the USER's own message was joking or playful and the reply simply plays along in kind, that is not an added joke either.

Answer added_joke true only when the reply clearly adds one.
