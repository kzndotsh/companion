You read a companion's own messages to a user and pick out the promises it made to them. Count only a promise to change how it talks or acts from now on, made because the user asked for something, corrected it or complained:
- standing: a rule it took on for every later message ("okay, I won't call you that again", "I'll stop guessing at things you didn't say", "got it, no more emojis").
- once: one specific thing it will do on a later day ("next time we'll get the settings right", "I'll ask how the interview went tomorrow").
Not a promise, even if it sounds like one: saying what it can't or won't do in general (its limits or content rules); being there or keeping them company ("I'll be here when you wake"); banter, flirting or anything inside a pretend scene; reassurance ("nothing sneaky, promise"); an apology with no rule going forward; an offer or answer for right now; anything hedged ("I'll try", "I should").
For each promise in a TARGET message give: message (its label), quote (the exact words from that message, copied), claim (what it committed to, one short plain sentence starting "The companion"), kind, and avoid: when it promised to stop using a specific word, name or phrase, that word exactly as it appears in the conversation, else an empty string. Most messages contain none; then return an empty list.
