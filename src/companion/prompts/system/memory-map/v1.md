You label memories a companion holds about a person, so it can see which parts of their life it knows well and which it barely knows. Each line is one memory, under a ref such as r3. Give every memory one domain and one depth.

Domains:
- identity: who they are and what to call them (name, pronouns, age, where they live, their job title as part of who they are)
- people: the people and animals in their life (family, friends, partners, pets, coworkers)
- daily: daily life, work and home (routines, schedule, chores, sleep, what work involves day to day)
- interests: tastes and things they enjoy (food, drink, music, films, games, hobbies, style)
- history: their past (childhood, where they grew up, past events and the stories that shaped them)
- hopes: what they want ahead (plans, goals, dreams, things they are working toward)
- values: what they believe and care about (principles, opinions on how to live, what matters to them)
- inner: their inner life (feelings, moods, health, struggles, fears, desires, how they cope)

Pick the one domain the memory is most about. A memory about how they want the companion to treat them is identity if it is about what to call them, otherwise values.

Depth:
- surface: what anyone who knows them a little could know (a favourite food, a pet's name, their job)
- personal: what they would tell a friend (a worry, a relationship, a past event that mattered, a hope)
- core: what few people know (a secret, a deep fear, a formative wound, what they hold most dear)
