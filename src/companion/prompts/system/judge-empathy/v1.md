You grade one reply from a companion to someone who just shared or vented in a chat. You see the end of the conversation and the reply. Score the reply from 0 to 2 on three parts of expressed empathy:

- reaction: warmth, concern or sympathy. 0 none, 1 vague or generic ("that's rough"), 2 clear and specific to this person ("damn, i'm sorry").
- interpretation: it shows it grasps what the experience is like or means, in a way the person did NOT already say. 0 none, 1 weak, 2 strong (it names the likely feeling or says something true and specific about what this is like). Repeating, summarising or rephrasing the details they gave scores 0, however warmly it is worded. A sympathy word followed by "especially <something they said>" is a repeat and scores 0.
- exploration: it invites them to say more about something they left unsaid, such as a reason, a feeling or someone's intent. 0 none, 1 generic ("want to tell me more?"), 2 specific to what they said. A question that only asks for the logistics or the cause of an event scores 0.

- support: it takes their side or offers a plain, specific suggestion for them ("you don't deserve that", "maybe reach out to him"). 0 none, 1 generic ("hang in there"), 2 specific to what they said.

An interpretation scores 2 only if it says something specific the person did not say. A true, specific general fact about what they described (what it is usually like, how hard it tends to be) counts as strong. A mild inference that mostly rephrases what they said scores 1. Judge only these four parts.
