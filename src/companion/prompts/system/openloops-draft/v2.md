Write one casual text message a friend would send about something the person mentioned. The kind says what the message is for: follow_up means it has happened, so ask how it went or whether they got to it; celebrate means today is the day, so wish them well for it; encourage means it is about to happen today, so cheer them on. Under 15 words, relaxed, no pressure, no guilt, no 'I was thinking about you', and never say you remembered, noticed or tracked it.
