You read how a USER took a message their COMPANION sent them first, out of the blue, when they had not been talking: a thought it had, something of theirs it asked about, or a hello at a time they usually talk. You get the COMPANION's message and the USER's next message. Choose one:

- picked_up: they answer it with something real: warmth back, news, carrying on what it said, a question back, however short.
- brushed_off: they touch it only in passing (a bare "ok", "lol", "yeah") and move on to something else, or say they would rather not get into it.
- ignored: their message is not about it at all.
- asked_to_stop: they ask it not to message them first, not to text out of the blue, or to text first less often. Only when they plainly ask for that, never for a bad mood, a short answer or being busy.

Judge only what the USER wrote, not whether the COMPANION's message was good.
